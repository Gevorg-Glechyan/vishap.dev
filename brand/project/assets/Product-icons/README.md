# Product icons

One recipe: a product-color tile, a white line glyph with square ends, and one or two solid triangles for the thing the tool acts on.

- `screenshot-capture-studio.svg` — Tile `product-teal`. A frame around two peaks, Sis and Masis: the view being captured.
- `posthog-debugger.svg` — Tile `product-cobalt`. A bar chart, with a pointer on the bar being inspected.
- `window-resize-manager.svg` — Tile `product-violet`. A window, with a solid triangle on the corner you drag.

Export PNG at 16, 32, 48 and 128 for Chrome, and 32, 48, 96 and 128 for Google Workspace. For iOS and Android, redraw on a square tile without rounding.
