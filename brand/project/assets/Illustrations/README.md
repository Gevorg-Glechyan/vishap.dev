# Illustrations

Flat 2D pictures in the vocabulary of dragon carpets: one colored field, one wool border line, and a few motifs (the dragon, the octagon medallion, the diamond, the eight-pointed star, the zigzag water line). Straight edges only, one fill per shape.

- `hero-dragons.svg` — 960 × 400. Hub hero: two dragons guard a star, the classic dragon-carpet arrangement.
- `one-job.svg` — 320 × 320. Principle: one job per tool. One medallion, one star.
- `nothing-hidden.svg` — 320 × 320. Principle: a clear price. The dragon and its whole reflection.
- `kept-inside.svg` — 320 × 320. Principle: your data is not sold. Two dragons guard a diamond.
- `product-screenshot-capture-studio.svg`, `product-posthog-debugger.svg`, `product-window-resize-manager.svg` — 320 × 320. A product as a medallion in its own color, with its icon glyph.
- `state-empty.svg` — 320 × 320. Nothing here yet: an empty medallion.
- `state-not-found.svg` — 320 × 320. One diamond is missing from the row; the dragon looks back.
- `state-done.svg` — 320 × 320. The star is complete.
- `store-tile-window-resize-manager.svg` — 440 × 280. Chrome Web Store promo tile: the product color as the field.

Every file carries its own field, so it reads the same in both themes.
