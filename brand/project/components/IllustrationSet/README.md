# IllustrationSet

The full illustration set in one view: the hub hero, the three principles, one picture per product and the three states. Use a picture where a page would otherwise be empty or wordy; one per section at most.

- The consumer provides the place and the size. Show spot pictures at 160 to 320px square and the hero at full column width.
- Do not crop a picture inside its border line, recolor it, or place text on top of it.
- A new picture follows the rules under Illustration in the brand book and is generated by `gen.py`.
