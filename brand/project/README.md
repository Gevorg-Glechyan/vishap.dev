vishap is a one-person workshop that makes small, single-purpose tools: Chrome extensions, Google Workspace add-ons, mobile apps and web apps. A vishap is the dragon of Armenian myth. Armenian weavers have drawn it for centuries on vishapagorg, "dragon carpets": flat, angular dragons with serrated backs on a madder-red field. The brand takes its mark, its colors and its pictures from those carpets.

**The idea in one line:** small tools, made the way a good rug is made. One motif each, drawn plainly, built to last.

## Voice

- Write "vishap" in lowercase in running text, always. Capitals appear only in the wordmark and in `label` text.
- Lead with what the tool does, in one plain sentence, verb first: "Sets any window to an exact size."
- Name exact things: "1280 × 800", not "your preferred size".
- No hype words, no exclamation marks, no emoji, no urgency.
- Sign every product "by vishap". Never "powered by" or "a vishap product".
- The tone is serious and calm. The dragon is never cute, never fierce.

## Color

The pages are quiet and neutral. The carpet colors are the brand.

- Set pages on `ground` with `ink` text. Use `surface` for cards and frames, `line` for hairlines.
- Use `ink-muted` for descriptions and `ink-subtle` for labels and metadata.
- `madder` is the brand color. Use it as a fill: the icon tile, illustration fields, the dragon in the wordmark. For madder-colored text or links use `madder-text`.
- `indigo` is the second field color. `wool` is the dragon and every line on a field. `ochre` is for small stars and diamonds only. `rose` is for reflections on madder.
- The primary button is an `ink` fill with an `on-ink` label. On a product page it takes the product color with an `on-product` label.
- Each product owns one flat color (`product-teal`, `product-cobalt`, `product-violet`). A new product may take any color that reaches 4.5:1 against `on-product` and differs clearly from the existing ones and from `madder`.
- Focus rings are 2px solid `ink`, offset 2px.
- Never use `indigo` bare on the dark ground, where it disappears. Never set text in `ochre`.

## Type

- One family for everything: Schibsted Grotesk, loaded from Google Fonts (weights 400, 500, 600, 700, 800).
- Headlines use `display`, `h1` and `h2`. Follow a headline with one `lead` sentence in `ink-muted`.
- Running text is `body`; cards and footers use `small`.
- Group labels and tags use `label`: uppercase, tracked, weight 700. This is the only uppercase text besides the wordmark.
- Use `mono` (JetBrains Mono) only for exact values: sizes, versions, shortcuts and dates.
- Keep running text under 65 characters per line. Left-align everything; nothing is centered except `Logos/vishap-stacked.svg`.

## Shape and layout

- The system is sharp. Use `radius-md` for buttons, inputs and cards, `radius-sm` for tags and `radius-lg` for screenshot frames. Only icon tiles are rounder (`radius-tile`).
- Depth comes from `surface` on `ground` and `line` hairlines. No shadows, no blur, no gradients.
- Pages are one column, at most 1080px wide, with a `space-12` gutter (`space-4` on phones) and `space-16` between sections.
- A product page opens with the icon, the name, one sentence, one button, then a real screenshot in a `surface` frame.

## Product icons

Every tool's icon follows one recipe, so the family is recognizable at 16px.

- **Tile:** a full-bleed square in the product color, rounded `radius-tile` on the web and in Chrome. Leave it square for iOS and Android masks.
- **Glyph:** `on-product` lines, 4.5 units on a 64 grid, square ends and sharp corners, inside the central 60%.
- **Subject:** one or two solid triangles for the thing the tool acts on: the view being captured, the bar being inspected, the corner you drag. Triangles are the dragon's own shapes.
- No curves, no second color, no text, and never the vishap mark inside a product icon.

Existing icons: `Product-icons/screenshot-capture-studio.svg` (a frame around two peaks), `Product-icons/posthog-debugger.svg` (a bar chart with a pointer on one bar), `Product-icons/window-resize-manager.svg` (a window and the corner you drag).

A product lockup is the icon, the product name in `h2`, and "by vishap" in `small` `ink-subtle` beneath it. Products that extend another company's platform add "(unofficial)" to the name.

## Illustration

Illustrations are flat and two-dimensional, drawn in the vocabulary of dragon carpets.

- **The field:** every picture is one flat field of `madder` or `indigo` (or the product color for a store tile) with one `wool` border line set in by `space-3`.
- **The motifs:** the dragon, the octagon medallion, the diamond, the eight-pointed star (a square over a diamond) and the zigzag water line. Build pictures only from these.
- **Flat means flat:** one fill per shape. No gradients, shadows, textures, facets, perspective or outlines around filled shapes.
- **Straight edges only:** every edge is horizontal, vertical or diagonal. No curves.
- **Symmetry:** compose around the vertical axis, as a carpet does. In illustrations the dragon may be mirrored to face the center.
- **Color roles:** the dragon and lines are `wool`. Stars and diamonds are `ochre`. A medallion is the opposite field color, or the product color when the picture is about a product.
- **A product picture** is its medallion: an octagon in the product color with the icon glyph at double size, and the dragon above it.
- **Restraint:** one main motif per picture, and at most two dragons. Leave most of the field empty.
- Never draw swastikas or any four-armed hooked cross, although old carpets contain them. Never tile the dragon as a pattern.
