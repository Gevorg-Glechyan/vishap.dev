What it does

Three ways to capture

Capture the visible area, an area you select, or the whole page from top to bottom.

Edit before you save

Annotate and crop in a local editor that opens right after the capture.

Export on your computer

Save as PNG, JPEG, WebP or PDF. Captures are kept in an offline history.
